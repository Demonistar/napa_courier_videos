Icons are already generated and present in this directory:

  build/icon.ico   — Windows icon (16, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256 px)
  build/icon.icns  — macOS icon  (16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024 px)

Both were generated from the NAPA hexagon badge design:
  Yellow hexagon outline (#FFB300), white interior, navy "NAPA" text (#002366).

To regenerate (e.g. after a brand refresh):
  - Edit /tmp/napa-icon.svg (or recreate a new master SVG)
  - Re-run the generation pipeline documented in electron-app/README.md
  - Or use electron-icon-builder with a new 1024x1024 PNG master:
      npx electron-icon-builder --input=my-icon.png --output=build/
